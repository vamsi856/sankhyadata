FP Glass · Gen 1

They look like glasses and capture like a lab.

At 78 g, FP Glass weighs about the same as a pair of sunglasses. Participants forget they're wearing it, and that's what keeps their behaviour natural.

  • 1× rolling-shutter RGB (1408×1408, 110° FOV) and 4× global-shutter SLAM cameras
  • Binocular IR eye tracking at 90 Hz
  • Dual IMU (1 kHz), magnetometer, barometer, 7-mic array
  • On-device VIO/SLAM, hand tracking and real-time de-identification
  • 6+ hours of recording per charge, hot-swappable battery pack
  • Prescription lens inserts and three frame sizes
FP Rig · Pro

Built for shifts, not just sessions.

FP Rig mounts on a cap, hard hat or headband. It adds a stereo pair and a downward-facing hand camera so the full manipulation workspace stays in view, even when the wearer is working at the waist or below.

  • Stereo RGB pair, 2.4K at 30 fps or 1080p at 60 fps, for metric depth
  • Wide downward hand camera (155° FOV)
  • Optional wrist IMUs and haptic data gloves
  • 10-hour battery with swap-in-place hip pack
  • Fleet management: OTA updates, remote health and auto-upload over Wi-Fi or LTE
  • IP54-rated, wipe-clean, compatible with standard PPE

FP SDK

Start reading recordings in five lines of code.

The open-source Python and C++ SDK covers device control, recording playback, calibration and MPS-style machine perception outputs: trajectories, point clouds, gaze and hands.

Read the docs GitHub 
# pip install fpl-sdk
from fpl import Recording

rec = Recording.open("kitchen_0142.fpr")
for frame in rec.stream("rgb", with_=["gaze", "hands", "pose"]):
    gaze_px = frame.gaze.project(rec.calib["rgb"])
    joints  = frame.hands.right.joints_3d   # (21, 3) in world frame
    T_world_device = frame.pose             # SE(3), 1 kHz interpolated

rec.export("lerobot", out="./episodes")
FAQ

Device questions

Can I buy devices outright, or only lease them?

Both. Research labs usually buy kits through the research program. Enterprise data programs typically lease fleets of 25 to 1,000+ devices, with maintenance, replacements and fleet tooling included.

Do you support multiple wearers in one session?

Yes. Devices sync over PTP, so you can record multi-person collaboration or teacher–student demonstrations on a shared timebase. You can also add external cameras to the same session.

Is the raw data accessible, or only processed outputs?

Both. You own your recordings. You can pull raw sensor streams with full calibration, our processed perception outputs, or both.

How do you handle bystander privacy?

Faces, licence plates and screens are blurred on the device in real time, before anything is written to storage or uploaded. A visible LED shows when recording is on, and wearers carry signage and an opt-out process.
