Research kit

Apply for a research kit

Applications are open on a rolling basis to university labs, non-profits and industry research teams.

Each kit includes

  • FP Glass devices (2–10 per lab) with charging and prescription inserts
  • FP SDK, calibration tools and cloud machine-perception credits
  • Early access to new open datasets
  • A dedicated research liaison and a partner Slack channel
Thanks for applying. We'll review your application and reply within three weeks.
