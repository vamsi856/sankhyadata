What we do

Hardware, operations and data under one roof.

Most teams have to stitch together a sensor vendor, a field-ops agency and an annotation shop. We handle the whole pipeline, from the first frame to a training-ready dataset.

Our glasses and head-mounted rigs are light enough to wear all day. Multi-camera RGB, eye tracking, IMUs, spatial audio and on-device SLAM are all clocked to one timebase.

How a program runs

From task spec to training data in weeks.

Scope

Together we define the tasks, environments, participant demographics and labels your model needs.

Deploy

We ship calibrated devices and train collectors in the right settings, whether homes, kitchens or factory lines.

Capture & QA

Live dashboards track coverage, sync health and quality. Bad sessions get flagged and re-collected.

Deliver

You receive annotated, de-identified episodes in your format, with a datasheet and full provenance.

Responsible by design

Consent, privacy and fair pay aren't add-ons.

Collecting first-person data from real people in real places carries responsibility. We built our process around that.

  • Informed, revocable consent for every participant, plus bystander signage and opt-out
  • On-device blurring of faces, licence plates and screens before data leaves the glasses
  • Participants are compensated at or above local living wage
  • Encrypted at rest and in transit, regional data residency, SOC 2–aligned controls
  • A datasheet with every dataset, covering demographics, environments and known gaps

FP Blur is our open-source de-identification model and runs on the glasses in real time. Learn more
